About Us

Dedicated to Thermotechnical Excellence

AB-Refractory Sh.p.k operates in Kosovo and specializes in the maintenance and construction of thermotechnical aggregates. Our primary aim is to support industrial companies as they initiate and sustain production.

With a workforce of 18 experienced professionals — scalable to meet any project requirement — we have earned visibility and trust across the region, partnering with companies in Albania, North Macedonia, and Greece.

Operations & Capabilities

01
Operations

Industrial Sectors We Serve

AB-Refractory specializes in the projection, construction, repair, and maintenance of various fireproof walls for thermotechnical aggregates across multiple industries.

  • Black metallurgy
  • Color metallurgy
  • Power plants
  • Cement industry
  • Heating systems
  • Beer industry
02
Fire Protection

Industrial Fire Protection Works

Our team executes comprehensive fire protection operations on industrial equipment, ensuring compliance and longevity across the most demanding environments.

  • Lining with refractory materials
  • Concreting with refractory concrete
  • Phase erection (special shapes)
  • Assembly formwork (wood, steel, aluminium)
  • Welding anchor installation
  • Cutting of refractory materials
03
Materials

Refractory Material Applications

We work with the full range of certified refractory materials, applying each with precision to guarantee maximum performance and durability under extreme thermal conditions.

  • Refractory brick (Silicate, High Alumina, Magnezi)
  • Thermo concrete & Insulation concrete
  • Plastics ramming mass
  • Gunning mass
  • Refractory mortar
  • Refractory reinforcement (inox)
04
Acid Protection

Acid & Chemical Protection

In the acid protection segment, AB Refractory delivers full surface treatment and chemical resistance solutions for industrial tankers and structures.

  • Sand-blasting of tankers & metal constructions
  • Coating with anti-acid bricks
  • Coating with anti-acid panels
  • Coating with polyester / polyurethane
  • Coating with epoxy
  • Anti-acid asphalt for chemical industry
  • Hydro-protection of foundations
